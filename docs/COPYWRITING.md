# Ad copy and reported clicks

Two examples connect the writing approach to its campaign's measured response. **The displayed post copy is adapted and anonymized for this portfolio.** Names, locations, exact project specifications, contact details and distinctive identifying phrases are omitted or rewritten. It is not a verbatim transcript of the live ad. Campaign figures retain their original values.

## Campaign 15 · Project story

**Post copy — anonymized adaptation**

> **One rooftop. More possibility.**
>
> A recent residential solar installation turns rooftop space into a source of energy for the home. Considering solar? Explore what a system could look like for your property and how it fits your household's priorities.

**Reported paid response**

| Link clicks | Ad spend | Cost per link click | Link CTR | Landing-page views |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **66** | **$19.88** | **$0.30** | **3.18%** | **32** |

**The writing approach.** The original post opened with a short, rhythmic project hook, described a completed installation, and linked technical project details to homeowner priorities. The adaptation above retains that structure while removing identifying specifications and wording. The invitation addresses people already considering the service.

**What the figures establish.** The original campaign copy was observed in the Business Suite ad preview. The campaign export reports 66 link clicks against $19.88 in ad spend. Cost per link click is $19.88 ÷ 66, rounded to cents. Link CTR is 66 ÷ 2,075 impressions × 100. The post text, image, audience, placement and delivery all contribute to a campaign; these figures do not isolate the effect of the words alone.

The adapted text shown here has not been run as a new ad. Its association is with the original campaign's verified copy, not a claim that this rewritten version generated those clicks. [Inspect Campaign 15](CASES.md#campaign-15).

## Campaign 17 · Direct service offer

**Post copy — anonymized adaptation**

> **Your next step starts with a conversation.**
>
> Speak directly with the team that reviews your roof, plans the system and handles the installation. Find out what a solar project could look like for your home. Request a free assessment.

**Reported paid response**

| Link clicks | Ad spend | Cost per link click | Link CTR | Landing-page views |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **90** | **$34.92** | **$0.39** | **2.17%** | **54** |

**The writing approach.** The original promoted post used a local installation as its opening context, emphasized working directly with the contractor team, explained the service from review through installation, and ended with a free-assessment call to action. This adaptation keeps the direct offer and clear next step while removing the geographic and identifying language.

**What the figures establish.** The original wording was observed in the matching Business Suite ad row alongside its $34.92 spend and 54 landing-page views. The corresponding export row supplies 90 link clicks and 4,155 impressions. Cost per link click is $34.92 ÷ 90, rounded to cents. Link CTR is 90 ÷ 4,155 × 100. The primary result for this campaign is landing-page views; link clicks are a separate reported metric.

The adapted text shown here has not been run as a new ad. This is a separate campaign example, not a controlled test against Campaign 15. [Inspect Campaign 17's expense](EXPENSES.md).

## Reading the results

Click counts are Meta-reported campaign outcomes. They are not sales, unique customers or proof that the copy caused the response. Landing-page views are shown separately and are not added to clicks. Revenue and sales conversion are unverified. Original wording and source observations are retained privately for reconciliation; the public presentation contains no ad images or source-post links.
