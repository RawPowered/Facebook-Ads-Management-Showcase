## Source and reporting dates

The source is two authenticated Meta Ads Manager CSV exports captured on October 24, 2026: the Performance preset (Source A) and Performance and clicks preset (Source B). The export included the option to include deleted campaigns with delivery data. No campaign was launched, edited, paused or published during this review.

Both downloaded files contain 21 campaign records and explicitly report **September 24, 2023 through September 4, 2026**. The screen's Maximum preset displayed September 24, 2023 through October 24, 2026. The export dates are retained as the coverage of the published figures. The discrepancy is unresolved. No later-period performance or complete lifetime history is claimed. The exported campaign end dates range from December 2025 to August 2026. These are schedule dates, not verified spend-by-day dates.

The accessible account is the entire evidentiary scope. Other business portfolios did not expose accessible ad accounts in the selector. Company count and performance at unavailable companies are unverified. Campaign aliases do not imply distinct companies. The repository represents selected, verifiable work across campaign periods.

## Reconciliation

Corresponding source rows were checked for matching campaign name, primary count, primary result indicator, spend, impressions, reach and end date. Duplicate-looking campaign names were retained as separate source records, never merged by name. The published aliases identify those individual rows. Source row includes the CSV header as row 1.

Spend reconciles to $2,521.39 across 21 records. Every exported record remains in the expense ledger. Four records contain no primary result value and represent $36.94 in spend. Their primary result is unavailable, not a measured zero. The two exports are duplicate reporting views of the same records and are not added together.

Cost per primary result = matching campaign spend / reported primary results. Group cost per result = matching group spend / matching group results. Link CTR = link clicks / impressions × 100. Display values are rounded to two decimals. The source attribution setting is **7-day click or 1-day view**. Source metric names and attribution do not establish causal incremental impact.

The comparison chart separates link clicks, landing-page views and messaging conversations. It does not imply equivalent business value. Views in Business Suite are not substituted for Ads Manager impressions. Different reporting surfaces showed different delivery counts, so all published numerical tables consistently use Source B.

## What the expense figures include

Figures are reported platform ad spend in USD. They are not invoice reconciliation, cash paid, taxes, refunds, creative production costs, management fees or total acquisition cost. Actual invoice expenses were not verified. Schedule end dates cannot be used to invent monthly spend allocations. No revenue, closed-won sales, profit, customer turnover, ROAS or lead-to-sale rate was established by these exports. Demonstrated efficiency is limited to cost per platform-reported response.

## Selection and management approach

Four examples are featured for the lowest observed cost within each primary result category and the highest messaging result count. Larger and less efficient spends remain available for inspection. The portfolio demonstrates expense reconciliation, objective-specific evaluation, source validation and privacy-conscious reporting. It does not invent a historical optimization sequence, A/B test or causal account of decisions that these exports cannot substantiate.

## Anonymization and evidence

Public data contains neutral campaign aliases and only necessary metrics, coverage and campaign end dates. Original campaign names, company/page names, advertiser IDs, personal names, creative copy, URLs, phone numbers, payment information and customer data are omitted. No authentic Meta screenshot containing those details is published. The graphics are **anonymized visualizations reconstructed from the verified export**, not unedited screenshots of Meta.

Raw CSV files and identity-bearing screen captures remain outside this repository. [Source fingerprints](../data/provenance.json) record SHA-256 values for the retained private files and their record counts. A fingerprint identifies a file for later comparison; it does not independently authenticate the platform or let a public reader verify a withheld source. The public dataset supports independent arithmetic checks within the published scope.
